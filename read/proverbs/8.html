Proverbs 8

King James Version · 36 verses · Explore cross-references interactively

1Doth not wisdom cry? and understanding put forth her voice?
See also: Proverbs 1:20–21
2She standeth in the top of high places, by the way in the places of the paths.
3She crieth at the gates, at the entry of the city, at the coming in at the doors.
4Unto you, O men, I call; and my voice is to the sons of man.
See also: Matthew 11:15John 3:16Psalms 50:1
5O ye simple, understand wisdom: and, ye fools, be ye of an understanding heart.
See also: Proverbs 1:22
6Hear; for I will speak of excellent things; and the opening of my lips shall be right things.
See also: Colossians 1:26Matthew 7:28–29
7For my mouth shall speak truth; and wickedness is an abomination to my lips.
See also: Psalms 37:30
8All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing froward or perverse in them.
See also: John 7:46
9They are all plain to him that understandeth, and right to them that find knowledge.
See also: Proverbs 14:6James 1:5John 7:17
10Receive my instruction, and not silver; and knowledge rather than choice gold.
See also: Proverbs 2:4–5Psalms 119:127Psalms 119:72
11For wisdom is better than rubies; and all the things that may be desired are not to be compared to it.
See also: Proverbs 4:5–7Proverbs 3:14–15Proverbs 16:16
12I wisdom dwell with prudence, and find out knowledge of witty inventions.
See also: Isaiah 28:26Exodus 35:30–36:4Romans 11:33
13The fear of the Lord is to hate evil: pride, and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the froward mouth, do I hate.
See also: Psalms 97:10Proverbs 16:61 Samuel 2:3
14Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom: I am understanding; I have strength.
See also: Ecclesiastes 7:19Isaiah 9:6Romans 11:33–34
15By me kings reign, and princes decree justice.
See also: Romans 13:1Daniel 2:21Matthew 28:18
16By me princes rule, and nobles, even all the judges of the earth.
17I love them that love me; and those that seek me early shall find me.
See also: John 14:23John 14:21Psalms 91:14
18Riches and honour are with me; yea, durable riches and righteousness.
See also: Proverbs 3:16Matthew 6:33
19My fruit is better than gold, yea, than fine gold; and my revenue than choice silver.
See also: Proverbs 10:20Proverbs 3:14
20I lead in the way of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of judgment:
See also: John 10:27–28Psalms 32:8Deuteronomy 5:32
21That I may cause those that love me to inherit substance; and I will fill their treasures.
See also: 1 Peter 1:4Proverbs 24:4Matthew 25:46
22The Lord possessed me in the beginning of his way, before his works of old.
See also: Colossians 1:17John 1:1–2Proverbs 3:19
23I was set up from everlasting, from the beginning, or ever the earth was.
See also: John 17:24John 17:5Micah 5:2
24When there were no depths, I was brought forth; when there were no fountains abounding with water.
See also: 1 John 4:9Proverbs 3:20
25Before the mountains were settled, before the hills was I brought forth:
See also: Psalms 90:2
26While as yet he had not made the earth, nor the fields, nor the highest part of the dust of the world.
27When he prepared the heavens, I was there: when he set a compass upon the face of the depth:
See also: Isaiah 40:22Job 26:10Proverbs 3:19
28When he established the clouds above: when he strengthened the fountains of the deep:
29When he gave to the sea his decree, that the waters should not pass his commandment: when he appointed the foundations of the earth:
See also: Psalms 104:9Jeremiah 5:22Genesis 1:9–10
30Then I was by him, as one brought up with him: and I was daily his delight, rejoicing always before him;
See also: John 1:1–3John 1:18Matthew 3:17
31Rejoicing in the habitable part of his earth; and my delights were with the sons of men.
See also: Psalms 16:3John 13:1
32Now therefore hearken unto me, O ye children: for blessed are they that keep my ways.
See also: Luke 11:28Psalms 119:1–2Psalms 128:1
33Hear instruction, and be wise, and refuse it not.
See also: Proverbs 4:1Psalms 81:11–12Proverbs 5:1
34Blessed is the man that heareth me, watching daily at my gates, waiting at the posts of my doors.
See also: Luke 11:28Matthew 7:24Proverbs 3:13
35For whoso findeth me findeth life, and shall obtain favour of the Lord.
