Proverbs 5

King James Version · 23 verses · Explore cross-references interactively

1My son, attend unto my wisdom, and bow thine ear to my understanding:
See also: Proverbs 22:17Revelation 2:11Proverbs 4:20
2That thou mayest regard discretion, and that thy lips may keep knowledge.
See also: Malachi 2:6–7Proverbs 16:23Proverbs 15:7
3For the lips of a strange woman drop as an honeycomb, and her mouth is smoother than oil:
See also: Proverbs 2:16Proverbs 6:24Proverbs 7:21
4But her end is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a twoedged sword.
See also: Ecclesiastes 7:26Psalms 57:4Psalms 55:21
5Her feet go down to death; her steps take hold on hell.
See also: Proverbs 7:27
6Lest thou shouldest ponder the path of life, her ways are moveable, that thou canst not know them.
See also: Proverbs 4:26
7Hear me now therefore, O ye children, and depart not from the words of my mouth.
See also: Proverbs 4:1
8Remove thy way far from her, and come not nigh the door of her house:
See also: Ephesians 5:11Proverbs 7:25Matthew 6:13
9Lest thou give thine honour unto others, and thy years unto the cruel:
10Lest strangers be filled with thy wealth; and thy labours be in the house of a stranger;
11And thou mourn at the last, when thy flesh and thy body are consumed,
See also: Romans 6:21Revelation 22:15
12And say, How have I hated instruction, and my heart despised reproof;
See also: Proverbs 12:1Proverbs 15:5Proverbs 1:25
13And have not obeyed the voice of my teachers, nor inclined mine ear to them that instructed me!
14I was almost in all evil in the midst of the congregation and assembly.
See also: Proverbs 13:20
15Drink waters out of thine own cistern, and running waters out of thine own well.
See also: 1 Corinthians 7:2–5Hebrews 13:4Proverbs 5:18–19
16Let thy fountains be dispersed abroad, and rivers of waters in the streets.
See also: Psalms 128:3Psalms 127:3
17Let them be only thine own, and not strangers' with thee.
18Let thy fountain be blessed: and rejoice with the wife of thy youth.
See also: Ecclesiastes 9:9Malachi 2:14–15Song of Solomon 4:15
19Let her be as the loving hind and pleasant roe; let her breasts satisfy thee at all times; and be thou ravished always with her love.
See also: Song of Solomon 7:3Song of Solomon 8:14Song of Solomon 4:5
20And why wilt thou, my son, be ravished with a strange woman, and embrace the bosom of a stranger?
21For the ways of man are before the eyes of the Lord, and he pondereth all his goings.
See also: Hebrews 4:13Proverbs 15:3Jeremiah 32:19
22His own iniquities shall take the wicked himself, and he shall be holden with the cords of his sins.
23He shall die without instruction; and in the greatness of his folly he shall go astray.
