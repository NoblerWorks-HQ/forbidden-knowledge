1The words of king Lemuel, the prophecy that his mother taught him.

2What, my son? and what, the son of my womb? and what, the son of my vows?
See also: Isaiah 49:151 Samuel 1:11
3Give not thy strength unto women, nor thy ways to that which destroyeth kings.
See also: Deuteronomy 17:17Nehemiah 13:26Proverbs 7:26–27
4It is not for kings, O Lemuel, it is not for kings to drink wine; nor for princes strong drink:
See also: Proverbs 20:1Isaiah 5:22Ecclesiastes 10:17
5Lest they drink, and forget the law, and pervert the judgment of any of the afflicted.
6Give strong drink unto him that is ready to perish, and wine unto those that be of heavy hearts.

7Let him drink, and forget his poverty, and remember his misery no more.

See also: Psalms 82:3–4Proverbs 24:11–12
9Open thy mouth, judge righteously, and plead the cause of the poor and needy.
See also: Isaiah 1:17Amos 5:11–12Leviticus 19:15
10Who can find a virtuous woman? for her price is far above rubies.
See also: Proverbs 19:14Proverbs 12:4Ephesians 5:25–33
11The heart of her husband doth safely trust in her, so that he shall have no need of spoil.
See also: 1 Peter 3:1–7
12She will do him good and not evil all the days of her life.
13She seeketh wool, and flax, and worketh willingly with her hands.
See also: Titus 2:51 Timothy 5:102 Thessalonians 3:10–12
14She is like the merchants' ships; she bringeth her food from afar.
15She riseth also while it is yet night, and giveth meat to her household, and a portion to her maidens.

16She considereth a field, and buyeth it: with the fruit of her hands she planteth a vineyard.

17She girdeth her loins with strength, and strengtheneth her arms.
See also: Job 38:31 Peter 1:13Ephesians 6:10

See also: 1 Thessalonians 2:9Matthew 25:3–10

20She stretcheth out her hand to the poor; yea, she reacheth forth her hands to the needy.
See also: Ephesians 4:28Hebrews 13:16Proverbs 22:9
21She is not afraid of the snow for her household: for all her household are clothed with scarlet.
22She maketh herself coverings of tapestry; her clothing is silk and purple.

23Her husband is known in the gates, when he sitteth among the elders of the land.

25Strength and honour are her clothing; and she shall rejoice in time to come.

29Many daughters have done virtuously, but thou excellest them all.
