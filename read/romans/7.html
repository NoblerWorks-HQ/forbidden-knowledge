1Know ye not, brethren, (for I speak to them that know the law,) how that the law hath dominion over a man as long as he liveth?
See also: Romans 6:14Romans 7:6Romans 6:3
2For the woman which hath an husband is bound by the law to her husband so long as he liveth; but if the husband be dead, she is loosed from the law of her husband.
See also: Genesis 2:23–241 Corinthians 7:41 Corinthians 7:39
3So then if, while her husband liveth, she be married to another man, she shall be called an adulteress: but if her husband be dead, she is free from that law; so that she is no adulteress, though she be married to another man.
See also: Exodus 20:14Matthew 5:32Mark 10:6–12
4Wherefore, my brethren, ye also are become dead to the law by the body of Christ; that ye should be married to another, even to him who is raised from the dead, that we should bring forth fruit unto God.
See also: 1 Peter 2:24Galatians 5:22–23Isaiah 54:5
5For when we were in the flesh, the motions of sins, which were by the law, did work in our members to bring forth fruit unto death.
See also: Ephesians 2:3Galatians 5:24Galatians 5:16–17
6But now we are delivered from the law, that being dead wherein we were held; that we should serve in newness of spirit, and not in the oldness of the letter.
See also: Galatians 3:23–252 Corinthians 3:6Romans 7:4
7What shall we say then? Is the law sin? God forbid. Nay, I had not known sin, but by the law: for I had not known lust, except the law had said, Thou shalt not covet.
See also: Romans 3:20Romans 4:151 Corinthians 15:56
8But sin, taking occasion by the commandment, wrought in me all manner of concupiscence. For without the law sin was dead.
See also: Romans 7:111 Corinthians 15:56Romans 4:15
9For I was alive without the law once: but when the commandment came, sin revived, and I died.
See also: Galatians 3:10Romans 8:7James 2:10–11
10And the commandment, which was ordained to life, I found to be unto death.
See also: 2 Corinthians 3:7Leviticus 18:5Galatians 3:12
11For sin, taking occasion by the commandment, deceived me, and by it slew me.
See also: Genesis 3:13
12Wherefore the law is holy, and the commandment holy, and just, and good.
See also: 1 Timothy 1:8Psalms 119:137Psalms 19:7–12
13Was then that which is good made death unto me? God forbid. But sin, that it might appear sin, working death in me by that which is good; that sin by the commandment might become exceeding sinful.
See also: James 1:13–15Galatians 3:21
14For we know that the law is spiritual: but I am carnal, sold under sin.
See also: 2 Kings 17:171 Kings 21:25Romans 7:18
15For that which I do I allow not: for what I would, that do I not; but what I hate, that do I.
See also: Galatians 5:17Romans 7:19–20Ecclesiastes 7:20
16If then I do that which I would not, I consent unto the law that it is good.

17Now then it is no more I that do it, but sin that dwelleth in me.
See also: Romans 7:20Philippians 3:8–9Romans 7:18
18For I know that in me (that is, in my flesh,) dwelleth no good thing: for to will is present with me; but how to perform that which is good I find not.

19For the good that I would I do not: but the evil which I would not, that I do.
See also: Romans 7:15
20Now if I do that I would not, it is no more I that do it, but sin that dwelleth in me.
See also: Romans 7:17
21I find then a law, that, when I would do good, evil is present with me.

22For I delight in the law of God after the inward man:

23But I see another law in my members, warring against the law of my mind, and bringing me into captivity to the law of sin which is in my members.

24O wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me from the body of this death?
