Job 37

King James Version · 24 verses · Explore cross-references interactively

1At this also my heart trembleth, and is moved out of his place.
2Hear attentively the noise of his voice, and the sound that goeth out of his mouth.
See also: Job 37:5Job 36:33
3He directeth it under the whole heaven, and his lightning unto the ends of the earth.
See also: Matthew 24:27Job 38:13
4After it a voice roareth: he thundereth with the voice of his excellency; and he will not stay them when his voice is heard.
5God thundereth marvellously with his voice; great things doeth he, which we cannot comprehend.
See also: Job 5:9Romans 11:33Isaiah 40:28
6For he saith to the snow, Be thou on the earth; likewise to the small rain, and to the great rain of his strength.
7He sealeth up the hand of every man; that all men may know his work.
See also: Psalms 111:2
8Then the beasts go into dens, and remain in their places.
See also: Psalms 104:22
9Out of the south cometh the whirlwind: and cold out of the north.
10By the breath of God frost is given: and the breadth of the waters is straitened.
See also: Job 38:29–30Psalms 147:16–18
11Also by watering he wearieth the thick cloud: he scattereth his bright cloud:
12And it is turned round about by his counsels: that they may do whatsoever he commandeth them upon the face of the world in the earth.
See also: Psalms 148:8
13He causeth it to come, whether for correction, or for his land, or for mercy.
See also: 1 Kings 18:45Job 38:26–271 Samuel 12:18–19
14Hearken unto this, O Job: stand still, and consider the wondrous works of God.
See also: Psalms 145:10–12Psalms 111:2Exodus 14:13
15Dost thou know when God disposed them, and caused the light of his cloud to shine?
16Dost thou know the balancings of the clouds, the wondrous works of him which is perfect in knowledge?
See also: Job 36:4
17How thy garments are warm, when he quieteth the earth by the south wind?
18Hast thou with him spread out the sky, which is strong, and as a molten looking glass?
See also: Isaiah 44:24
19Teach us what we shall say unto him; for we cannot order our speech by reason of darkness.
20Shall it be told him that I speak? if a man speak, surely he shall be swallowed up.
21And now men see not the bright light which is in the clouds: but the wind passeth, and cleanseth them.
22Fair weather cometh out of the north: with God is terrible majesty.
See also: Psalms 104:1
23Touching the Almighty, we cannot find him out: he is excellent in power, and in judgment, and in plenty of justice: he will not afflict.
See also: 1 Timothy 6:16Romans 11:33Job 36:5
24Men do therefore fear him: he respecteth not any that are wise of heart.
