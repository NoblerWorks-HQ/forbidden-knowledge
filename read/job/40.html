Job 40

King James Version · 24 verses · Explore cross-references interactively

1Moreover the Lord answered Job, and said,
2Shall he that contendeth with the Almighty instruct him? he that reproveth God, let him answer it.
See also: 1 Corinthians 2:16Isaiah 40:14Romans 11:34–36
3Then Job answered the Lord, and said,
4Behold, I am vile; what shall I answer thee? I will lay mine hand upon my mouth.
See also: Proverbs 30:32Job 29:9Job 21:5
5Once have I spoken; but I will not answer: yea, twice; but I will proceed no further.
See also: Psalms 62:11Job 33:14
6Then answered the Lord unto Job out of the whirlwind, and said,
See also: Job 38:1
7Gird up thy loins now like a man: I will demand of thee, and declare thou unto me.
See also: Job 38:3Job 42:4
8Wilt thou also disannul my judgment? wilt thou condemn me, that thou mayest be righteous?
See also: Romans 3:4
9Hast thou an arm like God? or canst thou thunder with a voice like him?
10Deck thyself now with majesty and excellency; and array thyself with glory and beauty.
See also: Psalms 93:1
11Cast abroad the rage of thy wrath: and behold every one that is proud, and abase him.
See also: Daniel 4:37
12Look on every one that is proud, and bring him low; and tread down the wicked in their place.
13Hide them in the dust together; and bind their faces in secret.
See also: Isaiah 2:10
14Then will I also confess unto thee that thine own right hand can save thee.
15Behold now behemoth, which I made with thee; he eateth grass as an ox.
16Lo now, his strength is in his loins, and his force is in the navel of his belly.
17He moveth his tail like a cedar: the sinews of his stones are wrapped together.
18His bones are as strong pieces of brass; his bones are like bars of iron.
19He is the chief of the ways of God: he that made him can make his sword to approach unto him.
20Surely the mountains bring him forth food, where all the beasts of the field play.
21He lieth under the shady trees, in the covert of the reed, and fens.
22The shady trees cover him with their shadow; the willows of the brook compass him about.
23Behold, he drinketh up a river, and hasteth not: he trusteth that he can draw up Jordan into his mouth.
24He taketh it with his eyes: his nose pierceth through snares.
