Job 33

King James Version · 33 verses · Explore cross-references interactively

1Wherefore, Job, I pray thee, hear my speeches, and hearken to all my words.
2Behold, now I have opened my mouth, my tongue hath spoken in my mouth.
3My words shall be of the uprightness of my heart: and my lips shall utter knowledge clearly.
4The Spirit of God hath made me, and the breath of the Almighty hath given me life.
See also: Job 32:8Genesis 2:7Job 27:3
5If thou canst answer me, set thy words in order before me, stand up.
6Behold, I am according to thy wish in God's stead: I also am formed out of the clay.
See also: Job 4:19
7Behold, my terror shall not make thee afraid, neither shall my hand be heavy upon thee.
See also: Job 13:21
8Surely thou hast spoken in mine hearing, and I have heard the voice of thy words, saying,
9I am clean without transgression, I am innocent; neither is there iniquity in me.
See also: Job 16:17Job 10:7
10Behold, he findeth occasions against me, he counteth me for his enemy,
11He putteth my feet in the stocks, he marketh all my paths.
See also: Job 13:27
12Behold, in this thou art not just: I will answer thee, that God is greater than man.
13Why dost thou strive against him? for he giveth not account of any of his matters.

14For God speaketh once, yea twice, yet man perceiveth it not.

23If there be a messenger with him, an interpreter, one among a thousand, to shew unto man his uprightness:

30To bring back his soul from the pit, to be enlightened with the light of the living.
