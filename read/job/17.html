Job 17

King James Version · 16 verses · Explore cross-references interactively

1My breath is corrupt, my days are extinct, the graves are ready for me.
See also: Isaiah 38:10–14
2Are there not mockers with me? and doth not mine eye continue in their provocation?
3Lay down now, put me in a surety with thee; who is he that will strike hands with me?
4For thou hast hid their heart from understanding: therefore shalt thou not exalt them.
See also: Matthew 11:25Matthew 13:112 Samuel 17:14
5He that speaketh flattery to his friends, even the eyes of his children shall fail.
See also: Psalms 12:2–3
6He hath made me also a byword of the people; and aforetime I was as a tabret.
See also: Psalms 44:14Job 30:9
7Mine eye also is dim by reason of sorrow, and all my members are as a shadow.
8Upright men shall be astonied at this, and the innocent shall stir up himself against the hypocrite.
9The righteous also shall hold on his way, and he that hath clean hands shall be stronger and stronger.
See also: Proverbs 4:18Isaiah 1:15–16Job 22:30
10But as for you all, do ye return, and come now: for I cannot find one wise man among you.
11My days are past, my purposes are broken off, even the thoughts of my heart.
See also: Job 7:6Isaiah 38:10
12They change the night into day: the light is short because of darkness.
13If I wait, the grave is mine house: I have made my bed in the darkness.
14I have said to corruption, Thou art my father: to the worm, Thou art my mother, and my sister.
15And where is now my hope? as for my hope, who shall see it?
16They shall go down to the bars of the pit, when our rest together is in the dust.
See also: Job 3:17–19
