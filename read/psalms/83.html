Psalms 83

King James Version · 18 verses · Explore cross-references interactively

1A Song or Psalm of Asaph. Keep not thou silence, O God: hold not thy peace, and be not still, O God.
See also: Psalms 28:1Psalms 35:22
2For, lo, thine enemies make a tumult: and they that hate thee have lifted up the head.
See also: Psalms 81:15
3They have taken crafty counsel against thy people, and consulted against thy hidden ones.
See also: Psalms 64:2Psalms 27:5Psalms 31:20
4They have said, Come, and let us cut them off from being a nation; that the name of Israel may be no more in remembrance.
See also: Jeremiah 48:2Jeremiah 11:19Psalms 74:8
5For they have consulted together with one consent: they are confederate against thee:
See also: Psalms 2:2
6The tabernacles of Edom, and the Ishmaelites; of Moab, and the Hagarenes;
7Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalek; the Philistines with the inhabitants of Tyre;
See also: Joshua 13:5
8Assur also is joined with them: they have holpen the children of Lot. Selah.
See also: Deuteronomy 2:9
9Do unto them as unto the Midianites; as to Sisera, as to Jabin, at the brook of Kison:
10Which perished at En–dor: they became as dung for the earth.
11Make their nobles like Oreb, and like Zeeb: yea, all their princes as Zebah, and as Zalmunna:
See also: Judges 7:25
12Who said, Let us take to ourselves the houses of God in possession.
See also: 2 Chronicles 20:11
13O my God, make them like a wheel; as the stubble before the wind.
See also: Psalms 35:5Job 21:18
14As the fire burneth a wood, and as the flame setteth the mountains on fire;
See also: Deuteronomy 32:22
15So persecute them with thy tempest, and make them afraid with thy storm.
See also: Job 9:17
16Fill their faces with shame; that they may seek thy name, O Lord.
17Let them be confounded and troubled for ever; yea, let them be put to shame, and perish:
See also: Psalms 35:4
18That men may know that thou, whose name alone is Jehovah, art the most high over all the earth.
See also: Exodus 6:3Ezekiel 38:23Jeremiah 16:21
