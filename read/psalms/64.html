Psalms 64

King James Version · 10 verses · Explore cross-references interactively

1To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David. Hear my voice, O God, in my prayer: preserve my life from fear of the enemy.
See also: Psalms 143:1–3Psalms 34:4
2Hide me from the secret counsel of the wicked; from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity:
See also: Psalms 143:9Psalms 109:2–3Psalms 59:2
3Who whet their tongue like a sword, and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words:
See also: James 3:6–8Jeremiah 9:3Psalms 58:7
4That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.
5They encourage themselves in an evil matter: they commune of laying snares privily; they say, Who shall see them?
See also: Psalms 140:5Psalms 10:11
6They search out iniquities; they accomplish a diligent search: both the inward thought of every one of them, and the heart, is deep.
See also: Matthew 26:59Jeremiah 17:9–10Isaiah 29:15
7But God shall shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly shall they be wounded.
See also: Proverbs 6:15Psalms 7:12–131 Thessalonians 5:2–3
8So they shall make their own tongue to fall upon themselves: all that see them shall flee away.
See also: Proverbs 18:7Proverbs 12:13Psalms 140:9
9And all men shall fear, and shall declare the work of God; for they shall wisely consider of his doing.
See also: Jeremiah 51:10Hosea 14:9Psalms 40:3
10The righteous shall be glad in the Lord, and shall trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glory.
