Psalms 110

King James Version · 7 verses · Explore cross-references interactively

1A Psalm of David. The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou at my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool.
See also: Hebrews 10:12–131 Corinthians 15:25Luke 20:42–43
2The Lord shall send the rod of thy strength out of Zion: rule thou in the midst of thine enemies.
3Thy people shall be willing in the day of thy power, in the beauties of holiness from the womb of the morning: thou hast the dew of thy youth.
See also: Judges 5:2Hebrews 13:21Titus 2:14
4The Lord hath sworn, and will not repent, Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchizedek.
See also: Hebrews 7:17Hebrews 5:6Hebrews 7:21
5The Lord at thy right hand shall strike through kings in the day of his wrath.
See also: Romans 2:5Psalms 16:8Revelation 17:12–14
6He shall judge among the heathen, he shall fill the places with the dead bodies; he shall wound the heads over many countries.
See also: Psalms 68:21
7He shall drink of the brook in the way: therefore shall he lift up the head.
