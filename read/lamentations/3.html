King James Version · 66 verses · Explore cross-references interactively

1I am the man that hath seen affliction by the rod of his wrath.
See also: Psalms 88:7
2He hath led me, and brought me into darkness, but not into light.
3Surely against me is he turned; he turneth his hand against me all the day.
4My flesh and my skin hath he made old; he hath broken my bones.
See also: Isaiah 38:13Psalms 51:8
5He hath builded against me, and compassed me with gall and travail.
See also: Lamentations 3:19
6He hath set me in dark places, as they that be dead of old.
See also: Psalms 88:5–6
7He hath hedged me about, that I cannot get out: he hath made my chain heavy.
See also: Job 19:8
8Also when I cry and shout, he shutteth out my prayer.
See also: Job 30:20Psalms 22:2
9He hath inclosed my ways with hewn stone, he hath made my paths crooked.
10He was unto me as a bear lying in wait, and as a lion in secret places.
11He hath turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces: he hath made me desolate.
See also: Hosea 6:1
12He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow.
See also: Psalms 38:2
13He hath caused the arrows of his quiver to enter into my reins.
14I was a derision to all my people; and their song all the day.
See also: Jeremiah 20:7
15He hath filled me with bitterness, he hath made me drunken with wormwood.
See also: Jeremiah 9:15
16He hath also broken my teeth with gravel stones, he hath covered me with ashes.
See also: Proverbs 20:17
17And thou hast removed my soul far off from peace: I forgat prosperity.
18And I said, My strength and my hope is perished from the Lord:
19Remembering mine affliction and my misery, the wormwood and the gall.
See also: Lamentations 3:15
20My soul hath them still in remembrance, and is humbled in me.
21This I recall to my mind, therefore have I hope.
See also: Habakkuk 2:3Psalms 130:7
22It is of the Lord's mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not.
See also: Psalms 86:15Luke 1:50Nehemiah 9:31
23They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.
See also: Hebrews 10:23Psalms 30:5Isaiah 33:2
24The Lord is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him.
See also: Psalms 73:26Psalms 16:5Psalms 142:5
25The Lord is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul that seeketh him.
See also: Micah 7:7–8Isaiah 40:31Psalms 130:5–6
26It is good that a man should both hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the Lord.
See also: Psalms 37:7Psalms 37:34Psalms 130:5
27It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth.
See also: Ecclesiastes 12:1Matthew 11:29–30Psalms 119:71
28He sitteth alone and keepeth silence, because he hath borne it upon him.
See also: Jeremiah 15:17Lamentations 2:10
29He putteth his mouth in the dust; if so be there may be hope.
30He giveth his cheek to him that smiteth him: he is filled full with reproach.
See also: Isaiah 50:6Job 16:10Matthew 26:67
31For the Lord will not cast off for ever:
See also: Psalms 94:14Psalms 103:8–10Psalms 77:7
32But though he cause grief, yet will he have compassion according to the multitude of his mercies.
See also: Psalms 78:38Psalms 30:5Psalms 103:11
33For he doth not afflict willingly nor grieve the children of men.
See also: Ezekiel 33:11Ezekiel 18:32
34To crush under his feet all the prisoners of the earth,
See also: Zechariah 9:11–12
35To turn aside the right of a man before the face of the most High,
36To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord approveth not.
37Who is he that saith, and it cometh to pass, when the Lord commandeth it not?

38Out of the mouth of the most High proceedeth not evil and good?

41Let us lift up our heart with our hands unto God in the heavens.
See also: Psalms 25:1Psalms 28:2

43Thou hast covered with anger, and persecuted us: thou hast slain, thou hast not pitied.
See also: Lamentations 2:21Lamentations 2:17

46All our enemies have opened their mouths against us.
See also: Lamentations 2:16Psalms 22:6–8
47Fear and a snare is come upon us, desolation and destruction.

49Mine eye trickleth down, and ceaseth not, without any intermission,
50Till the Lord look down, and behold from heaven.

56Thou hast heard my voice: hide not thine ear at my breathing, at my cry.

57Thou drewest near in the day that I called upon thee: thou saidst, Fear not.

60Thou hast seen all their vengeance and all their imaginations against me.

61Thou hast heard their reproach, O Lord, and all their imaginations against me;
62The lips of those that rose up against me, and their device against me all the day.
See also: Ezekiel 36:3
63Behold their sitting down, and their rising up; I am their musick.
