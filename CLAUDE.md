# Forbidden Knowledge (repo `forbidden-knowledge`, was `versegraph` until 2026-11-02)

Nobler Works open-source demo, branded "Forbidden Knowledge" since 2026-11-01: lost civilizations, secret societies, banned books and Bible codes, each claim checked against sources (cross-refs, scroll timeline, ladder + daily quiz, myths, theories, map, extra-canonical texts + readers, papal timeline and legends, Egypt/Masons/Rosicrucians, Secret Traditions lineage chart, Dante's afterlife, honest ELS search). Prototype; no cloud.

- `site/index.html` is the whole app; `site/data/` is generated by `scripts/build_data.py` (gitignored).
- Published as artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/DfW1Z7Bpsi1CB5m8SgeTXn - republish `site/index.html` with every `site/data/*` file (17, incl. `world.json` and `library.json`) and every `site/img/*.jpg` (140).
- Keep the OpenBible CC BY attribution in the footer. Code Breaker always shows chance baselines; never present ELS hits as meaningful.
- Social previews: `site/og/*.jpg` (1200x630, committed) from `scripts/build_og.py` (Pillow: `~/.cache/motion-studio-venv/bin/python`); re-run after adding a section or editing `VIEW_INFO`. `build_site.py` makes `/<section>/` share pages that use them.
- `site/img/` holds public-domain Wikimedia Commons images only (checked license, resized to 900px max); credit each in `IMG` in index.html.
  Exception: `site/img/app-*.jpg` are our own products' social images (the home page app strip).
- Myth and canon cards are factual claims: verify against the text (or a cited source) before adding; evidence counts are computed live.
- Public site: `scripts/deploy-pages.sh` -> gh-pages -> forbidden.noblerworks.com (Route 53 CNAME in NW, since 2026-11-02). The old versegraph. record is deleted.
- CI: `.github/workflows/ci.yml` on free Actions (public repo) builds data + site and checks the inline JS; it never deploys.
